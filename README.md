# Kapitza Pendulum Dynamic Stabilization Platform

**Nonlinear Dynamics | Mechanical Design | Experimental Prototyping**

## Overview

This project involved designing, fabricating, and testing a laboratory-scale Kapitza pendulum to demonstrate **dynamic stabilization of an inverted pendulum through high-frequency vertical excitation**. The platform translates a nonlinear dynamics phenomenon into a physical electromechanical system, combining a motor-driven excitation mechanism with a mechanically reinforced structure for repeated cyclic operation.

## Problem Statement

An inverted pendulum is ordinarily unstable under gravity. However, sufficiently rapid oscillation of its pivot can stabilize the inverted configuration. The objective was to develop an experimental platform that could generate controlled, sustained oscillatory motion and demonstrate this counterintuitive behavior in practice.

## Engineering Approach

- **Mechanical design:** Modeled the pendulum assembly and experimental platform in SolidWorks, accounting for mechanism motion, component alignment, and structural support.
- **Actuation:** Designed the platform around a **24 V, 350 W, 3000 RPM brushed permanent-magnet DC motor** to drive the excitation mechanism.
- **Motion conversion:** Developed a custom **crank-slider mechanism** to convert motor rotation into the reciprocating motion required for pendulum excitation.
- **Structural reliability:** Incorporated a reinforced frame, linkage-driven components, heat-set inserts, and vibration-resistant design features to withstand repeated oscillation.
- **Fabrication and testing:** Manufactured components using additive manufacturing, assembled the electromechanical system, and conducted experimental testing of the inverted pendulum response.

## Results and Outcomes

The completed experimental platform successfully demonstrated **vibration-induced stabilization of the inverted pendulum**. The assembled system operated under continuous cyclic loading, providing a physical demonstration of a nonlinear dynamics concept and practical experience in mechanism design, fabrication, and experimental validation.

## Tools and Methods

- SolidWorks
- Mechanical mechanism and linkage design
- Crank-slider motion conversion
- Nonlinear dynamics and dynamic stabilization
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing)
- Electromechanical assembly and experimental testing

## Key Engineering Takeaway

The project illustrates how carefully designed periodic excitation can change the stability of a mechanical system. Building the apparatus required integrating nonlinear dynamics theory with practical considerations such as actuator selection, alignment, vibration resistance, and durability.
